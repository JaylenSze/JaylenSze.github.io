Mic Drop Madness is a real-time karaoke battle game built with PyGame. It compares live audio against the original track to give each singer a score from 0 to 10, then sends the results to the Gemini API for personalized feedback on their singing. It placed 2nd of 23 teams at the Lockdown 2025 high school hackathon.
